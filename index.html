Zuzalu

Zuzalu is a global movement that began as a pop-up city experiment, bringing together pioneers in technology, public goods, longevity, governance, and decentralized networks to explore new models of co-living, co-working, and collaboration. ‍ ‍

More than just an event, Zuzalu is a growing network of interconnected communities, fostering open innovation, shared knowledge, and real-world impact.  ‍

As part of this movement, 4Seas provides a lasting hub for cypherpunks, digital nomads, and visionaries to connect, build, and experiment—extending Zuzalu’s legacy in Chiang Mai.

What is 4Seas

A cultural hub in Chiang Mai,
rooted in Ethereum and part of the Zuzalu Movement.
We are actively building the future of community, work, and coordination, leveraging decentralized technologies to do so —
where these pioneering concepts seamlessly transition into a lived, daily reality.

Coliving

Our coliving is built around shared daily life, where people from different backgrounds live, work, and spend time together naturally.
It fosters a lifestyle where genuine human connection emerges through everyday interactions, shared experiences, and simply being together—within a space that balances focus and openness: private rooms for longer stays and shared areas for deep connection, continuously shaping a shared culture.

Coworking

Our co-working space is designed for both focused work and cross-disciplinary exchange.
Flexible work areas and quiet zones allow for different working rhythms, while dedicated phone booths and meeting rooms create space for private discussions and small group collaboration. Bringing together global innovators, the space enables ideas to be developed, tested, and refined in real time. Reliable, high-speed Wi-Fi keeps work uninterrupted.

Event Space

Our event space is designed for gatherings of different scales, from small discussions to larger community events.
It supports talks, workshops, and meetups across a wide range of topics, from technology and blockchain seminars to meditation and social gatherings, bringing together diverse perspectives and fostering intellectual growth.
To host an event, please contact TK, via linktr.ee/tk4seas.

Exhibition Space

Our exhibition space is where ongoing explorations in crypto, culture, and future societies are made visible, at the intersection of technology and philosophy.
It brings together projects, experiments, and narratives in a curated space, allowing ideas to be seen, interpreted, and engaged with—opening new perspectives and extending conversations beyond discussion.

Content Studio

Our content studio is where ideas, conversations, and narratives emerging from the community are captured and shared beyond the space.
It supports recording, filming, and storytelling in a focused environment, allowing voices and perspectives to be documented, refined, and extended—carrying insights and discussions beyond physical gatherings.
