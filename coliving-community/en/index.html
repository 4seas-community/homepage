CHIANG MAI & NIMMAN

City life, with nature close by.

Make a home in Chiang Mai and a connection with local life.

Why Chiang Mai?

  • A well-known base for digital nomads
  • A relaxed rhythm with room for work and life
  • Mountains, waterfalls and national parks, many around an hour away
  • International ideas, rooted in Lanna hospitality

Why Nimman?

  • A central Chiang Mai neighbourhood
  • Meetups, workshops and pop-up markets nearby
  • Walk to coffee, local food and everyday essentials
  • Around 15 minutes by car to the airport, depending on traffic
